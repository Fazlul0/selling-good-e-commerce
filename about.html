Our story

A small shop with a long view.

Northwind Goods started in 2017 in a converted boat-repair loft in Portsmouth, New Hampshire. We design, source, and ship everyday objects built to last — and we tell you, plainly, where each one comes from.

How we work

Every product in our shop comes from a workshop we've visited. We carry eight to ten lines at a time, and we replace a line only when the maker can no longer meet our standards. Most of our suppliers are family-run, two or three people deep, and we've been working with the same ones since the first year.

We don't run sales. We don't release limited drops. We price honestly, and we mark up just enough to keep the lights on, pay our team, and set aside a small fund for repair or replacement if something arrives less than perfect.

What we believe

  • Fewer, better. A single object used for a decade beats a closet of replacements.
  • Honest materials. If we can't tell you where something was made and by whom, we don't carry it.
  • Quiet design. Nothing here is loud, branded, or trying to date itself. Form follows function, and then we leave it alone.
  • Repair first. If something breaks, we'd rather fix it than sell you a new one.

The team

A four-person shop. We answer the phones, pack the orders, and write the copy ourselves.
